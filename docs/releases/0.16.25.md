InkRead 0.16.25 adds saved reports for articles that do not extract correctly.

- Open an article's Article Source menu and choose Report extraction problem. Add a note, review the diagnostic preview, then save it for later, copy it, or share it with an app of your choice.
- Reopen saved reports under Settings → Troubleshooting → Extraction reports. Reports stay with the account in which you saved them and remain available after restarting InkRead.
- Reports include the sanitized public article URL, app and device version, requested and displayed source, and known details of the last local extraction attempt. They distinguish fetch/access/resource errors from successful extraction that was rejected by the content quality check, such as a missing photograph.
- Credentials, account identifiers, private feed links and captured article text are excluded. Saving a report does not fetch the article again or change its source preference, read/star state or displayed content. Earlier attempts that InkRead did not observe remain explicitly unknown.

Includes the New Yorker photograph, summary and older-cache fixes from 0.16.24. Extraction policies and access restrictions are unchanged.

Validated with 898 automated unit tests, four Android UI tests, release lint/build checks, and three optimized Android 11 runtime checks including real-file report save, reopen, sanitized export and removal. Physical BOOX Palma Pro acceptance remains a device check.
