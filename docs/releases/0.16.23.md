InkRead 0.16.23 improves article sources and reading progress while preserving existing accounts, saved articles, annotations and subscription settings.

- Improve full-text reading for public Verge and New Yorker articles, preserving article endings, images, citations and links.
- Remember Local full text, Server content or Original RSS separately for each subscription. Existing settings keep their prior behavior until a source is chosen.
- Original RSS reads the subscription feed anonymously. If an older article is no longer available in that feed, useful current text stays visible. Newsletter privacy controls remain in force.
- Keep the newspaper list at the current article when Next or Previous crosses a page boundary.

Subscriber-only and blocked articles still require browser access. Very large pages may reach existing local reading limits. Server content depends on the feed and server settings and may still be a summary.

Validated with automated extraction, source-selection, persistence and navigation checks, plus Android 11 runtime tests. Physical e-ink/mouse behavior and live account acceptance still need device verification.
