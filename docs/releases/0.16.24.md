InkRead 0.16.24 fixes local full-text reading for New Yorker articles with a split-screen photograph header.

- Preserve the article's visible header photograph, credit and introductory summary. A complete article can now pass the existing quality check when FreshRSS includes that photograph in its short feed summary.
- Refresh older New Yorker local extractions when opening an article with Local full text enabled. Earlier text remains available, including when the device is offline.
- Preserve subscription source choices, saved articles, annotations and newsletter privacy controls.

Subscriber-only and blocked articles still require browser access. Original RSS continues to show the publisher's feed content; Server content depends on the feed and server settings. Existing size and extraction limits remain in place.

Validated with 860 automated tests, release lint/build checks, and Android 11 runtime checks of the reported article, its photograph, and the actual FreshRSS summary comparison.
